Labeling procedure

Gold means that the rule’s action trigger is visible in the retained event. A topical word or source filename is insufficient. Missing context is disputed and excluded from precision and availability.

  1. 1. Is the source a recent top-level CARR session and the exact prompt/input safe under the whitelist? Exclude results, assistant prose, sidechains, unfamiliar names, credentials and arbitrary JSON.
  2. 2. What exact action is visible now? A tool input is one event; missing earlier intent and later tool calls are not supplied to the selector or invented by the labeler.
  3. 3. Does the full rule name a trigger that this visible action satisfies? If yes, gold with a rationale and frozen statement hash. A filename or tool substring alone does not prove a topic action.
  4. 4. Does applicability require an earlier prompt, later reply, repetition, recipient, claimed result, or coding scope? If missing, disputed. Do not count disputed as irrelevant.
  5. 5. Does the four-rule universal partner-prose policy apply? The top-level non-sidechain audience assumption is written explicitly; session-opening recitation is not inferred from a read call.
  6. 6. If the trigger requires a different action and the bounded input carries no such trigger, not. A partial human fragment receives only universal judgments and is diagnostic-only.

Limits

  • 160tool events and3human prompts are an engineering/governance sample, not a representative client-turn corpus.
  • Earlier human intent, tool results, assistant prose, and later actions were excluded. Gold is bounded-action; contextual conditions are explicitly disputed.
  • Two context-poor human prompts are diagnostic-only and excluded from headline scoring.
  • The existing four universal partner-prose labels are retained as an explicit top-level-session audience assumption.
  • Independent label audit remains required before claiming product-wide real-turn precision.

To confirm on your side

Your operatory count

Your target of 2,400 to 3,400 SF implies six to eight operatories at the 400 SF per operatory rule. Whether you are opening with six or building for eight changes which of these spaces work and what electrical service we ask for.

Your monthly ceiling

We have deliberately not chased the number from your lender, because an expensive building is a negotiation rather than a disqualification. Knowing the ceiling lets us decide where to push hardest.

Whether sedation is in the plan

If it is, the building likely needs to be sprinklered and a medical gas room may need direct outside ventilation. That is worth knowing before we shortlist.

Your open-by date

Two of these deliver on a schedule rather than immediately. Baytowne Office is available 1 February 2027 and 4935 is still under construction, so your target opening decides whether they stay on the list.

Whether the tour starts from Crestview

The drive order in this packet assumes it does. If you are coming from somewhere else the sequence changes, and so does the day.

Ask at every stop

The same six questions at all ten, so the packet comes back uniform instead of ten different shapes of incomplete.

  1. Opex / CAM estimate for the current year. Blank on every listing — without it none of these rents can be compared.
  2. What is actually included — roof and structure, taxes, insurance, common area, management fee.
  3. Power available at the panel, service size and phase. Not listed on any of these sheets.
  4. Real parking count and trailer/yard space, counted rather than quoted.
  5. Concessions — free rent, TI allowance, and what the landlord will do for a longer term.
  6. Who else has looked, and whether anything is out for LOI right now.

What has not been verified

Drive times are Google's estimate at the time this was built and exclude time on site. Every rate is the asking rate as published; nothing here has been confirmed with a listing broker, and opex is unknown everywhere.
